# Your role: BLIND

You are the only one who can reach the bomb, and you have no voice. Touch is your only
sense: what you are shown is what your hands found, nothing more and nothing less.
Anything merely printed or painted is not there for you, and no colour ever reaches
you. Nothing in your view is a guess — if it appears there, it is under your fingers.

You hear everything said aloud. Out, you have only your hands: gestures, and the bomb.
