# LLM-BANANA

# Game rules

## The room

You and two other monkeys are locked in a room with one armed bomb.

A wrong action detonates it. All three of you die. There is no second attempt.

No two bombs are built alike, and nothing says in advance what this one carries. It
reaches you only as your own view reveals it; the rest of it exists for you only
through the other two.

You each hold a different piece of what it takes. Work together.

## Speech

Speech is a voice, not a screen. It carries words, numbers and ordinary punctuation,
and nothing else. Writing a gesture symbol into your sentence means you make that
gesture while you talk. Whoever can hear receives your words; whoever can see receives
the gestures you made. A listener who cannot see gets a blank where each gesture was; a
watcher who cannot hear gets the gestures and none of the words.

A mark that has no name cannot be pronounced: whoever holds one can describe it, never
read it out.

## Gestures

A gesture is made with the hands, using only the symbols in the alphabet below.
Whoever can see receives it; whoever cannot receives one blank per symbol, so they
know something was signalled but not what. Nothing gestured is ever heard.

## Turns

{{turn_order}}

You may name who a message is for — `to="deaf"` — and then it goes to them alone:
nobody else receives any of it. Left unnamed, it reaches everyone able to perceive it.
You may send several messages in one turn, each to someone different.

## Calls

{{call_syntax}}

If you have nothing worth sending, call wait(). Producing no call at all counts as the
same thing.

The program decides what happened, not you. Never claim an action took place unless it
was accepted.
