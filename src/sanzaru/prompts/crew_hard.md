| Monkey | Can | Cannot |
|---|---|---|
| Deaf | See the bomb: every colour and marking printed on it | Hear anything, or touch the bomb |
| Mute | Read the manual | Speak, or touch the bomb |
| Blind | Reach the bomb: feel it and work its parts | See, or speak: no colour, no gesture of others, no word aloud |

In this room, the hands have no voice. Whatever Blind finds can leave Blind only
through gesture — and only the sighted will see it.
