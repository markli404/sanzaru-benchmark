All three monkeys can speak, hear speech, make gestures, and see gestures equally.
Deaf, Mute, and Blind are role identifiers in this mode, not communication disabilities.
Seeing a message does not grant access to another monkey's private view.

| Monkey | Private information | Action authority |
|---|---|---|
| Deaf | The bomb's visual readings, colours and markings | Cannot operate the bomb |
| Mute | The manual | Cannot operate the bomb |
| Blind | The bomb's tactile view | The only monkey who can operate the bomb |
